# OpenGameArt assets

- [Low Poly Weapon Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-weapon-pack) by byzmod3d, CC0. M16, sniper and RPG-7 meshes with game-specific materials and first-person placement.
- [Gunshot Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds) by Tabasco, CC0. SKS, Mosin Nagant and shotgun recordings edited to short mono clips for the three paint markers.
- [City Kit (Industrial)](https://opengameart.org/content/city-kit-industrial) by Kenney, CC0. Building A and two shipping container variants scaled to the arena.
